Privacy

Everything is stored locally in this browser. With the AI settings off (the default) nothing leaves your device. If you switch AI on and a service is connected: AI styling sends clothing details (name, category, colour, style, wear history) and your style profile, not photos; clothing analysis sends the photo you upload; weather sends your approximate location. Data goes to only to produce analysis, outfits and weather. Virtual Try-On sends the selected clothing images and model settings to , only after you press Generate Look and agree. You can turn each feature off at any time.

About: Wardrobe AI, version 1.0. Outfits are built by a built-in rule engine from clothes you add.
